### What to expect

- **Paid:** usable creative to test consistently, followed by iterations based on the data available. If you have no previous winners, the first batch establishes what to test.
- **Organic:** regular publishing that helps prospects discover your expertise, understand your offer and take the next step, with less production coordination on your side.
- **Both:** the organic publishing service and paid creative production, with specific decisions shared between them in the monthly review: which topics to develop, which ads to refresh and which objections to address next.

The commercial goal is more qualified enquiries and clients. Do not publish a guaranteed client count or a 30-day acquisition promise.
